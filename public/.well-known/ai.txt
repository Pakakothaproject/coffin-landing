# CoffinMail

> Mails After Death. Write your Death Mail now — a monthly check-in keeps it waiting, and it is only ever sent after months of writing to you first. Three months free, no credit card.

## What it is

CoffinMail is a dead man's switch for the words you would want said. You write letters now, in your own time, and name the people who should receive them. You check in once a month with a one-tap email. If you stop answering, the service writes to you ten times across eighty days before anything else happens — and if nobody ever gets a reply, the letters are delivered on day 92.

It is not a will, a probate step, or a determination of death. It determines nothing and certifies nothing.

## Pricing

$20 covers one message for one year. Every further message is $5. Every further year is $5 for every message being held. Nothing ever costs more than $500 — that is the price of the longest term, called Forever. Three months are free to start, with no credit card asked for at any point.

## Questions this site answers

- How long before anything is sent after I stop checking in? About five months from your last check-in; ten reminders over eighty days come first, and delivery is on day 92.
- Does anyone else get contacted? Only if you ask us to. Naming somebody is optional, is a single email with two buttons, and requires no proof of anything.
- Can your staff read my letters? No. Not an administrator, not a support agent. The words only move when they are delivered.
- Why do you need two email addresses? Because an address can quietly stop accepting mail. The second one is only written to after three quiet days.
- What happens when a term ends? One email a month beforehand, then the checking stops. Nothing renews and there is no card on file.
- Is this legal proof of death? No, and the site does not pretend otherwise. It is not a death certificate, not a probate step, and not a determination of anything.

## If the company ever stops

Ninety days' notice to every account. Everything exportable stays exportable, with no fee and no waiting period. No slot is ever converted into a charge. Remaining terms are refunded automatically. Every message already prepared and addressed is delivered before the service closes.
